## Where to find the owner's email (5-min per institute)

1. **Their website's Contact page** — look for `founder@`, `director@`, or `admin@[domain]`. Sometimes just `info@`.
2. **LinkedIn** — search `"[institute name] founder"` or `"[institute name] director"`. Free tier lets you send ~20 connect requests/week. Send a connect request first, message when accepted.
3. **Google Maps listing** — the phone number is almost always the owner's WhatsApp for smaller institutes.
4. **Justdial listing** — owner phone + email visible for most Tier-2/3 institutes.
5. **Their Facebook page** — the person who runs the FB page is usually the owner or their kid. Message from there.
